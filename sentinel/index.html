SETTINGS

Difficulty

Higher settings send warheads faster and in greater numbers, and from wave three some of them split partway down into several smaller ones.

Word set

Every warhead in the air gets a different starting letter, so a keypress is never ambiguous.

Keyboard guide

Fades a keyboard across the play field and lights up the next key, color-coded by the finger that should press it. When stuck keeps it hidden until you miss three keys in a row, then shows it until you get one right.
