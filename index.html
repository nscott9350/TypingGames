Built for practice

Every game here shares the same word sets, so you can drill a specific part of the keyboard and then take it into any of them. Grand Finale is the exception that proves it: made of single letters rather than words, the set stops being a filter and becomes the drill — choose Home row and only the home row falls on you.

  • Home row — anchor your fingers on asdf jkl;
  • Left hand / Right hand — isolate one side
  • Common words — the words you actually type most
  • All words — the full mixed vocabulary

Every run tracks WPM, accuracy and your best streak, and keeps a top-ten table per difficulty.

Or skip the drill sets entirely and let the Trainer pick for you — it watches which keys you are slow or inaccurate on and weights what it shows you toward those.

Built with plain HTML, CSS and canvas — no frameworks, no accounts. Visits are counted anonymously; nothing you type ever leaves your browser.
